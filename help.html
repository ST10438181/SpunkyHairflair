Help & Support

Answers to common questions about using Spunky's Hairflair

How do I book an appointment?

Log in or register, open Book Now, then choose a service, a stylist, a date and a time. Review the summary and tap Confirm Booking.

How much is the deposit?

A 50% deposit of the service price secures your slot. The balance is paid at the salon. The summary shows both amounts before you confirm.

Can I cancel an appointment?

Yes. Open My Bookings and press Cancel on the appointment. The slot becomes available to other customers again.

Why can't I pick a certain time?

Times already booked with your chosen stylist are greyed out, and times that have already passed are hidden. Choose another time or stylist.

When is the salon open?

Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. We are closed on Sundays, so Sundays are not offered when booking.

How do I change my details or password?

Click your name at the top right to open Settings. You can update your name and phone number, or change your password.

Who are the stylists?
