# Privacy Policy – Spunky's Hairflair app

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

This policy explains what the Spunky's Hairflair app ("the app") collects and why. The app is used to book appointments
at Spunky's Hairflair.

## What we collect
* **Account details:** your name, email address, phone number and password.
* **Booking details:** the service, stylist, date, time, price and deposit for each appointment you make.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising ID, and the app has no ads or analytics.

## How we use it
* To create your account and sign you in.
* To make, show and cancel your appointments and to contact you about them.

## How it is stored and protected
* Your data is sent to our server over HTTPS and stored in our database.
* Your password is never stored as plain text. It is stored as a salted hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256).
* The app keeps a sign-in token on your phone so you stay logged in. Logging out removes it.

## Sharing
We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties.

## Keeping and deleting your data
Your data is kept until you delete your account. To delete it, open the app, go to **Settings ▸ Delete my account** and enter
your password. This permanently deletes your account and all your bookings. You can also request deletion by emailing
hello@spunkyshairflair.co.za.

## Children
The app is not intended for children under 13.
